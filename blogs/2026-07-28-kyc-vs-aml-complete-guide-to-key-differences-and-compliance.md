In today’s complex regulatory environment, financial institutions and digital platforms must balance user experience with stringent compliance standards. Two of the most critical acronyms in this space are Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML). While often used interchangeably, these concepts represent distinct yet interconnected pillars of financial compliance.
